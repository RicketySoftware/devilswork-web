Devil's Work

Devil's Work is a card-based comedy RPG. You play as a horrible piece of incompetent scum who has been press-ganged into harvesting souls for Larry.

Introduction

Somewhere in the murk of the Widderplane, an aggressively strange universe with botched natural laws, a Devil lamented that he was exhausted.

The specific devil was "Larzzanaar, the phthalo prince of desperation, collector of bartered souls and the scourge of the second dominion-quadrata", known as "Larry" for short. He had spent the last seventeen centuries burning the candle at both ends. His business involved granting the wishes of desperate mortals in exchange for their precious souls, but fatigue eventually catches up to everyone, even the immortal. With tired eyes and an aching back, he perused the chambers of his personal hell. As he carefully counted the exotic artifacts of his impressive collection of souls, he quietly admitted to himself he needed some time off.

So he left his domain, took the form of a plain, kind, balding, middle-aged man and travelled on the Astrogale, the endless arcane wind that swirls around the worlds of the Widderplane, to the cube planet of Dirt, to convalesce. Dirt's faces were divided into six distinct dominions. His favourite familiar stomping ground, Pennylund, was the second dominion, populated with at least a dozen almost-intelligent types of humanoid.

His holiday had only just begun when his newfound tranquility was repeatedly pelted by the obnoxious habits and transgressions of the local denizens. No matter where he roamed on the square face of Pennylund, thick, horrible, selfish, oblivious people found him and intentionally or inadvertently ruined his day. After countless offenses, his blood began to boil. He would make sure they would pay for their crimes against his majesty. Furthermore, being the enterprising creature he was, he would also ensure their misery would not go to waste. He would put them all to work in his business; covering for him, granting the wishes of his clients to the best of their ability or dying trying. He would rest, and they would do the devil's work.

Each job is a one-shot that lasts 1-2 hours, comprising of roughly three connecting rooms full of collectable cursed items. Devil's Work is all about the story, so admirable roleplay is rewarded with Power Charges for your character.

In a Nutshell

What are you doing? Devil's Work is a card-based comedy roleplaying game.[cite: 1] One player, the Story God, runs the world; everyone else plays a pathetic stooge sent to do the Devil's work.

Premise: Somehow your character has offended Larry, a Devil, and now he is enacting his revenge upon you by forcing you to work for him, granting his clients' wishes in exchange for their souls. Each session is a one-shot: a new group of characters, a new client, a new wish, a new jobsite. Desperate clients have sold their souls as a last resort, and you're there to save them.

The Aim: In every job you will need to escape the clutches of Larry by surviving the task of satisfying the criteria of the client's wish. Once the wish has 'technically' been fulfilled, the client's soul will be harvested and you will be released from Larry's employment.

Job Failure: If you fail the objective or if every player experiences one death, you have failed and Larry will confiscate your souls instead of "letting you go".
