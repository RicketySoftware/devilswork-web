Devil's Work

Somewhere in the murk of the Widderplane, an aggressively strange universe with botched natural laws, a Devil lamented that he was exhausted. The specific devil was “Larzzanaar, the phthalo prince of desperation, collector of bartered souls and the scourge of the second dominion-quadrata”, known as "Larry" for short. He had spent the last seventeen centuries burning the candle at both ends. His business involved granting the wishes of desperate mortals in exchange for their precious souls, but fatigue eventually catches up to everyone, even the immortal. With tired eyes and an aching back, he perused the chambers of his personal hell. As he carefully counted the exotic artifacts of his impressive collection of souls, he quietly admitted to himself he needed some time off. So he left his domain, took the form of a plain, kind, balding, middle-aged man and travelled on the Astrogale, the endless arcane wind that swirls around the worlds of the Widderplane, to the cube planet of Dirt, to convalesce. Dirt’s faces were divided into six distinct dominions. His favourite familiar stomping ground, Pennylund, was the second dominion, populated with at least a dozen almost-intelligent types of humanoid. His holiday had only just begun when his newfound tranquility was repeatedly pelted by the obnoxious habits and transgressions of the local denizens. No matter where he roamed on the square face of Pennylund, thick, horrible, selfish, oblivious people found him and intentionally or inadvertently ruined his day. After countless offenses, his blood began to boil. He would make sure they would pay for their crimes against his majesty. Furthermore, being the enterprising creature he was, he would also ensure their misery would not go to waste. He would put them all to work in his business; covering for him, granting the wishes of his clients to the best of their ability or dying trying. He would rest, and they would do the devil’s work. Devil’s Work is a card-based comedy RPG. You play as a horrible piece of incompetent scum who has been press-ganged into harvesting souls for Larry. You must attempt to fulfill the impossible wishes of his clients to keep your soul, otherwise you will become a permanent part of his collection. Each job is a one-shot that lasts 1-2 hours, comprising of roughly three connecting rooms full of collectable cursed items. Devil’s Work is all about the story, so admirable roleplay is rewarded with Power Charges for your character. Characters are built quickly; randomly draw one card from three twelve-card decks to determine your personality, species, and skills. Just name your character, build a quick backstory, choose what your specific sin against Larry was and you're ready to work. The game uses a single, twelve-sided die (d12). When you roll to determine the success of an action, you combine the result with your relevant incompetency; which are all negative, because, as we mentioned previously, you are incompetent scum.
