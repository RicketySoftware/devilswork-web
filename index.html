Devil's Work

A card-based comedy RPG of incompetent mortals fumbling through impossible soul contracts.

The Premise

In the Widderplane, an aggressively strange universe with botched natural laws, a powerful devil named Lazzenar (or "Larry" for short) is exhausted from granting wishes and collecting souls. To take a vacation, he press-gangs pathetic, incompetent scum who wronged him into doing his job for him.

You play as one of these unfortunate stooges. Fulfill Larry's clients' impossible wishes in 1-2 hour one-shot missions, or fail and have your soul added permanently to his personal collection.
