How to Play

Player reference rules for surviving Larry's assignments.

1. Character Creation

Character creation takes less than two minutes. You draw three cards at random:

  • Heart: Your personality traits and your Critical Failure Table when rolling a natural 1.
  • Kin: Your species, physical traits, unique Kin Power, and your 6 Incompetency modifiers.
  • Class: Your job, free weapon attack, and unique Class Power.

Give your character a name, a quick backstory, and declare what minor slight or sin you committed against Larry to end up in Hell.

2. Rolling the d12 & Incompetencies

Whenever you attempt an action with an uncertain outcome, you roll a single d12 and combine the result with your relevant Incompetency (found on your Kin card):

Scrawniness: Throwing, Lifting, Tackling, Grappling

Sloth: Climbing, Swimming, Sprinting, Acrobatics

Infirmity: Breath Holding, Poison, Staying conscious

Repulsivity: Intimidating, Seducing, Insulting

Obliviosity: Spotting details, Reading people

Stupidity: Lock Picking, Investigating, Memory

Critical Success (Natural 12): Automatic spectacular success.
Calamity (Natural 1): Automatic failure. Consult your Heart Card to see what horror unfolds.

3. Turn Anatomy & Health

Your Turn in Combat: You can ACT + MOVE + SPEAK in any order.

  • Health Points: Everyone starts at 12 HP. At 0 HP, your character dies. Describe your death well to earn a Power Charge for your next character!
  • Power Charges: Earned through good roleplay or funny choices. Spend them to execute powers or multi-action Stunts.
  • Cursed Items: Single-use items earned during jobs. Roll to activate their power, then roll to see if the curse triggers.
